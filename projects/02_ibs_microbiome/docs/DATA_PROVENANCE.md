# Data provenance

Primary public source:
- BioProject: PRJNA637763
- SRA study: SRP266324
- Organism: human gut metagenome
- Assay: 16S rRNA amplicon
- Region: V1–V2
- Platform: Illumina MiSeq
- Layout: paired-end
- Public sequence volume: about 1.37 GB across 111 SRA experiments.

The publication reports 85 adult IBS patients and 26 healthy controls.

No IOM/company data, private files, or synthetic biological measurements are used.
